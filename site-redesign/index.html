our story

Designed so you can be present for the moments that matter.

At Blank Space, life's most meaningful moments are celebrated in a setting designed to feel both beautiful and effortless.

Created thoughtfully by an event planner, the venue gives Woodstock and Oxford County hosts a flexible room that can shift with each unique vision, from soft showers to polished milestone celebrations.

Because gatherings should be filled with joy, not stress, Blank Space is built to become the backdrop for laughter, happy tears, heartfelt toasts, and memories guests will carry home.
