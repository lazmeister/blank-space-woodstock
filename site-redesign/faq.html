questions

Answered

Good answers before you send the inquiry. Here are the details most hosts ask about before booking Blank Space for an event in Woodstock or Oxford County.

How many guests can Blank Space host?

Blank Space includes tables and chairs for up to 72 guests.

Where is the venue located?

Blank Space is at 159 Main Street in downtown Woodstock, Ontario, N4S 1T1.

Is parking available?

Yes. There are 9 onsite parking spots and additional street parking nearby.

Is the venue accessible?

The venue has an accessible entrance and two washrooms, including one accessible washroom.

What is included with a rental?

Tables, chairs, Wi-Fi, projector and screen, Bluetooth speaker, washrooms, coat racks, baby change table, fridge, dishwasher, coffee maker, kettle, water pitchers, and water goblets for 50.

Is there a kitchen?

There is a prep-friendly setup with fridge, dishwasher, coffee maker, and kettle. Confirm your catering needs when you inquire.

Are plates, cutlery, and linens included?

Core venue amenities are included, but dishes, cutlery, and linens should be confirmed or sourced separately unless arranged through an add-on.

Can we decorate?

Yes, but decoration rules apply. Ask before using anything that attaches to walls, ceilings, floors, or fixtures.

Does setup and cleanup count in my time?

Yes. Please include your setup and cleanup time when requesting your booking block.

What deposit is required?

A $200 damage deposit is required. Your date is secured after the contract and deposit are complete.

When will I hear back?

Availability requests are reviewed and responded to within 48 hours.

Can I tour before booking?

Yes. Use the contact page to request a tour or ask questions about your event.
