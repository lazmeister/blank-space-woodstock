# Blank Space Website Redesign Plan

Audit date: May 15, 2026  
Website reviewed: https://www.blankspacewoodstock.ca/

## Business Assessment

Blank Space is a boutique event venue in downtown Woodstock, Ontario at 159 Main Street. The business appears to serve intimate private and semi-private events: baby showers, bridal showers, birthdays, engagement parties, anniversary celebrations, intimate dinners, corporate gatherings, client appreciation events, information sessions, workshops, classes, and studio use.

The core offer is a 1,500 sq ft modern, open-concept rental venue with natural light, accessible entry, two washrooms, tables and chairs for up to 72 guests, Wi-Fi, parking, and simple add-on services. The brand is positioned around stylish, low-stress hosting rather than a bare community hall rental. The strongest differentiator is the combination of a chic neutral venue, event-planner-created experience, transparent pricing, and optional planning/decor/bar/soft play add-ons.

## Current Site Style

The existing site has a calm, editorial, boutique feel:

- Palette: warm off-white, dark espresso/charcoal, black typography, soft neutrals.
- Typography: large elegant serif headlines mixed with handwritten script logo/section accents.
- Mood: minimal, feminine, polished, event-focused, intentionally spacious.
- Imagery: venue photography and lifestyle/event imagery, though some media appears slow or fails to load as black placeholders in screenshots.
- Layout: very spacious sections, large headings, simple navigation, rounded dark CTA buttons.

The style is worth preserving, but it needs more structure, clearer user journeys, better mobile density, stronger image delivery, improved SEO targeting, and conversion paths that make the booking process feel obvious.

## Main Problems To Fix

- The homepage has charm but not enough immediate practical clarity. A visitor should understand capacity, location, best-fit events, pricing entry point, and booking path within seconds.
- The hero media appears as a black loading box in captured screenshots, which weakens trust and first impression.
- Mobile pages have too much vertical dead space and some CTAs wrap awkwardly.
- Pricing is useful but visually hard to compare. It needs a clearer package table or structured pricing section.
- SEO copy is too light for local intent. The site needs stronger Woodstock, Oxford County, and event-type landing content without becoming spammy.
- The site should guide three conversion paths: book a date, request a tour, and ask a planning/pricing question.
- FAQ content is useful but should be expanded to address common booking objections before users contact the business.
- Contact and booking pages should reduce uncertainty about response time, deposits, capacity, timing, setup, catering, alcohol, parking, accessibility, and what happens after submission.

## Redesign Direction

### Visual Thesis

Chic, warm, editorial, and light-filled: a modern Woodstock event venue with the polish of a boutique studio and the calm of an effortless celebration.

### Content Thesis

Lead with the venue's concrete value: a beautiful 1,500 sq ft event space in downtown Woodstock for showers, birthdays, workshops, and intimate celebrations, with tables, chairs, parking, Wi-Fi, and optional event support already thought through.

### Interaction Thesis

- Use graceful page transitions and section reveals that feel calm, not flashy.
- Let venue photography carry the brand through full-width editorial image moments.
- Make conversion controls sticky or repeated naturally on mobile: "Check Availability," "Request a Tour," and "View Pricing."

### The Space

Goal: help visitors picture the venue and know what is included.

Recommended sections:

- Photo-led intro.

- Included amenities.
- Accessibility and parking details.

  - Weekend full day

- Add-on services with clear outcomes and pricing.
- Planning and coordination service as a premium upsell.

- Soft Play and Party.

### FAQ

Goal: answer objections clearly and improve SEO with natural question language.

Add or improve FAQs around:

- How many guests can Blank Space host?
- Where is Blank Space located?
- Is parking available?
- Is the venue accessible?
- Can we bring our own food or catering?
- Is alcohol allowed, and what licensing or insurance is needed?
- What is included with a rental?
- Is setup and cleanup included in the time block?
- Can we decorate?
- What decorations are not allowed?
- Is there a kitchen?
- Do you provide dishes, linens, or glassware?
- When is my booking confirmed?
- What deposit is required?
- What happens after I submit a date request?
- Can I tour before booking?

### Contact

Goal: make low-commitment inquiries easy.

Recommended sections:

- Simple intro for tours, questions, and custom event requests.
- Contact form with event type, preferred date, guest count, and message.
- Email and phone as secondary options.
- Location block with map and parking note.
- Response expectation: within 48 hours.

### Book / Check Availability

Goal: turn ready visitors into inquiries.

Recommended sections:

- Clear "Request a Date" headline.
- Short explanation of process:
  1. Submit preferred date and start time.
  2. Blank Space confirms availability within 48 hours.
  3. Date is secured after contract and deposit.
- Booking form or embedded scheduler.
- Reminder that setup/cleanup time must be included.
- Secondary CTA: "Not ready? Request a tour."

- Write for people first. Use local phrases where they clarify location and service.
- Put "Woodstock, Ontario" and "Oxford County" in important headings and intros.

- Include alt text for venue images, such as "Blank Space event venue in Woodstock Ontario set for a bridal shower."

- Add a consistent NAP block: Blank Space, 159 Main Street, Woodstock, ON N4S 1T1, phone, email.

## Conversion Strategy

Primary conversion: check availability / request a date.  
Secondary conversion: request a tour.  
Tertiary conversion: ask a question or request an event planning quote.

Recommended CTA language:

- Check Availability
- Request a Tour
- View Pricing
- Ask About This Date
- Request a Planning Quote

Conversion improvements:

- Repeat CTAs after hero, pricing, event types, FAQ, and footer.
- Add a mobile sticky CTA bar with "Book" and "Call" or "Email."
- Keep booking steps visible near the form.
- Make pricing easy to scan before asking users to book.
- Use reassurance copy near forms: response within 48 hours, date secured after deposit and contract, setup/cleanup reminder.
- Add trust signals: real venue photos, event examples, testimonials if available, planning experience, included amenities, and clear policies.

## Accessibility And Mobile Requirements

- Ensure all text has strong contrast on off-white and dark backgrounds.
- Use real buttons/links with accessible labels.
- Make tap targets at least 44px high.
- Avoid CTA labels wrapping awkwardly on mobile.
- Reduce excessive mobile vertical gaps.
- Ensure navigation opens/closes reliably and is keyboard accessible.
- Preserve "Skip to Content."
- Add alt text to all meaningful images.
- Do not rely on script-style text for important information.
- Use responsive image sizes so mobile does not download oversized media.
- Test pages at common mobile widths: 390px, 430px, 768px, and desktop.

## Design System Notes

Preserve:

- Warm off-white background.
- Dark espresso/charcoal sections.
- Elegant serif typography.
- Handwritten logo/accent use.
- Rounded dark CTA buttons.
- Calm editorial whitespace.
- Chic neutral photography.

Improve:

- Use photography earlier and more confidently.
- Make the hero image/video reliable and fast.
- Build denser mobile layouts without losing luxury.
- Use pricing tables and clear comparison patterns.
- Use fewer huge empty vertical spaces.
- Create a consistent CTA system.
- Use a restrained accent color only if needed, such as muted champagne, warm clay, or soft olive, but keep the current neutral identity dominant.
